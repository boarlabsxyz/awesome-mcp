Connectors

Google Workspace

Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Gmail and Calendar. They are six separate connectors, but the setup is the same for all of them.

Six connectors share one page because they share one sign-in. Each is added separately from the dashboard and gets its own URL, but they all send you to the same Google consent screen and ask only for what that particular service needs.

Connecting

  1. On the dashboard, click + Add Tool and pick the Google service you want.
  2. You are sent to Google’s consent screen. Pick the account — if you have several, this is where you choose which one this connection uses.
  3. Review the permissions and approve.
  4. You land back on the dashboard with the new connection. Press Copy URL.

Adding a second Google service means going through consent again, because each one asks for different permissions. Adding the same service for a second Google account also works — you get two rows and two URLs.

Google connections expire

Unlike the other connectors, Google access is time-limited and refreshed in the background. If a refresh ever fails, the connection’s row shows Reconnect instead of Copy URL. Press it and approve again; the URL you already gave Claude keeps working.

Google Docs

Read, write and format documents; work with comments, tables and images. Asks for access to your documents and your Drive files.

  • “Summarize the doc at https://docs.google.com/document/d/…”
  • “Append a "Next steps" section to my planning doc”
  • “Find every "TODO" in this doc and turn them into a checklist”

Inserting images into a Doc is covered on the images page.

Google Sheets

Read and write ranges, append rows, look rows up by a column value, and run batch updates. Asks for access to your spreadsheets and your Drive files.

  • “Read the "Q3 forecast" sheet and chart revenue by region”
  • “Append a new row with today's standup notes”
  • “Clear the scratchpad range A1:D50”

Google Slides

Create and read presentations, edit slides and speaker notes. Asks for access to your presentations and your Drive files.

  • “Add a title slide to the kickoff deck”
  • “Replace "OLD_LOGO" with our new logo in every slide”
  • “Read the speaker notes from slide 5”

Google Drive

Browse, search, move, copy and share files and folders. Asks for access to your Drive files.

  • “Find the latest version of the pricing memo”
  • “Move all PDFs from /Inbox to /Archive”
  • “Share this folder with alex@ as commenter”

Gmail

Read, search, send and draft mail; create and apply labels; work with attachments. Asks for permission to read and modify your mail.

  • “Summarize unread mail from this week”
  • “Draft a reply to the latest message from finance@”
  • “Search for invoices from Stripe and label them "Receipts"”
Gmail can send

This connector can send mail as you, not only read it. If you would rather it only drafted, say so in your prompt — there is no server-side read-only mode.

Google Calendar

List calendars, read and create events, move and update them. Asks for access to your calendars and their events.

  • “What's on my calendar tomorrow?”
  • “Schedule a 30-min sync with the team for Thursday afternoon”
  • “Move my 4pm meeting to Friday”

Reading a busy week can return a lot of data. The REST API exists for exactly that case.
